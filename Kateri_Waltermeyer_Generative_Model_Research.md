# Executive summary

Over the last six months this programme produced working systems in five
areas that are usually pursued by separate teams: identity-preserving
personalisation, few-step distillation, 3D scene generation and editing,
video- model conditioning, and controllable music generation. Several
results have no published equivalent. The through-line is control ---
making large generative models do a specific, verifiable thing rather
than a plausible one. Three capabilities came out of that: A complete
photo → navigable 3D walkthrough stack with clean commercial provenance
end to end. A single photo becomes a 360° panorama, a metric point
cloud, and a photoreal video walkthrough along an author-chosen camera
path. The key training asset --- a licence- clean synthetic dataset of
\~2,200 camera-controlled clips with recovered ground-truth poses ---
was generated by the models themselves, not licensed. An editable,
persistent world representation. A panoramic scene is decomposed into a
peeled background layer plus amodal object layers with per-region
provenance, so edits (recolour, replace, complete) persist as world
state and survive a subsequent camera fly- through. The four distinct
edit operators were characterised and measured; the failure mode of each
is now diagnosable rather than mysterious. Few-step distillation on
models nobody had distilled. The first working port of NVIDIA's rCM
consistency-distillation onto a flow-matching video model (LTX-2.3), and
a video mixture-of- experts model (Wan2.2-A14B) distilled at T=1 into a
production-grade few-step image generator with a shipped ComfyUI
inference path. Alongside those, the identity work resolved a question
worth resolving honestly: auxiliary identity losses do not beat a
well-configured plain LoRA on easy prompts, and the real, unsolved
failure is subject bleed into crowds. That finding cost several sprints
and saves many more --- and it produced the two things that did earn
their keep: a decode-free latent identity supervisor that works where
pixel decoding is impossible (video), and a measurement stack that can
actually see the bleed axis the standard metric is blind to. A fourth,
less visible output is methodological. Across image, video, 3D, and
audio, the same lesson recurred: the convenient metric measures the
wrong thing. ArcFace cosine cannot see clone bleed. Laplacian variance
scores motion artifacting as detail. Aesthetic panels called an audio
checkpoint a regression that the ear called the breakthrough. Every
workstream now carries a purpose-built evaluator alongside the
convenient one, plus an explicit rule about which arbitrates.

N O V E L T Y Where we went past what the literature describes Results
with no published equivalent we could find, or where the published
version covers a materially different regime. IDENTITY · TRAINING
Decode-free latent identity loss for image personalisation A small
frozen probe reads the diffusion VAE latent of a face and emits a 512-d
identity embedding distilled from a pixel-space recogniser, used as an
auxiliary loss on the predicted-clean latent --- no VAE decode per step.
The mechanism is published for video full-finetune only (SemanticREPA);
applying it to image LoRA personalisation, and the whole
distillation-and-weighting recipe, is ours. Ported and validated on two
model families (Z-Image, Qwen-Image). DISTILLATION rCM consistency
distillation on a flow-matching video model No public rCM/sCM port
existed for any flow-matching video model --- TurboDiffusion covers Wan
only, and Lightricks ships an inference-only DMD-distilled LTX-2. Our
LTX- 2.3 port is novel, and root-causing its collapse to a double-scaled
timestep at the seam between rCM's Wan-conventioned wrapper and LTX's
native scaling is a transferable finding for anyone attempting the same.
DISTILLATION Video mixture-of-experts → few-step image generator
Wan2.2-T2V-A14B (two-expert MoE, boundary ratio 0.875) distilled at T=1
into a single 14B consistency student that learns the high expert above
the σ-boundary and the low expert below it, in one network, 2-step
inference. Production-grade 4- step output by \~2k iterations, with a
validated ComfyUI export path. Also produced the clean statement of what
distillation cannot do: it cannot change aesthetics, because the frozen
teacher is the target --- restyling has to happen upstream on the
teacher.

3D · DATA Camera control as an emergent property of point-track
conditioning LTX-2.3's Motion-Track IC-LoRA controls object motion via
image-space point tracks. By synthesising camera-consistent tracks ---
projecting a monocular depth cloud through a chosen trajectory so near
points move more than far ones --- the model reads real 3D parallax and
produces rigid camera motion it was never trained to produce. Validated:
pose recovery tracked 121/121 frames, straightness 0.998, per- frame
rotation mean 0.045°, trajectory rank ≈ 1-D. This turned an
object-motion tool into a licence-clean camera-controlled data
generator. VIDEO · CONDITIONING Attention partitioning turns
out-of-distribution multi-anchor into in- distribution sub-problems LTX
is in-distribution on image-to-video and start+end-anchor, and
out-of-distribution on arbitrary multi-keyframe. Rather than softening
the anchors, we partition self- attention into per-segment sub-problems
with a sigma-scheduled isolation relaxation, so every interior free
region looks like a training case the model has seen. That reframing ---
make the sub-problem familiar rather than make the constraint gentler
--- generalises to any anchored-generation setting. VIDEO · CONDITIONING
RoPE temporal distance as a conditioning-strength dial On MiniMax H3, a
conditioning row's effective strength is dominated by its RoPE distance
from the target's own frame 0. Anchoring a context block at literal zero
distance --- rather than at a "physically continuous" one-frame offset
--- is the difference between style/identity locking and merely echoing.
We then extended PackedLayout to chain multiple context blocks through
one continuously-decreasing RoPE index, giving two independent context
slots (real prior-clip continuation and room grounding at the same
time). MiniMax's own continuation system is proprietary and API-only; no
open implementation existed.

3D · EDITING An operator taxonomy for generative 3D scene editing Four
operators --- material change, inpaint/replace, complete-unseen, shape
edit --- differ in what holds geometry still, and using one for
another's job fails diagnosably. Measured: material edit via intact
pixels + depth ControlNet at denoise 0.9 gives 55.3% material change at
98.8% silhouette IoU; blank-and-refill for the same job redraws the
silhouette smaller and keeps 0.3% of original pixels; intact pixels at
denoise 0.55--0.75 preserve the outline and change nothing. We have not
seen this decomposition stated anywhere. 3D · REPRESENTATION Layered
world state as the fix for edit haloes A flat panorama can never repaint
a wall cleanly, because a ring of pixels around every occluder belongs
to neither the wall mask nor the object mask. The fix is structural, not
a better boundary heuristic: a peeled background layer where every
surface is complete behind the furniture, plus amodal object layers with
their own pixels and depth. Measured: wall plane region 319k → 707k px,
halo ring 12,550 px 100% painted, objects untouched, revert lossless.
One change subsumes haloes, object dragging, asset upload, and stale
masks after replacement.

## Audio

Separability-first data selection for stem-conditioned music training
The intuitive move --- select acoustic/classical/chamber tracks because
those are the failure cases --- selects exactly the population a source
separator cannot cleanly separate, so you train on noisy targets for the
hardest cases. Selecting by separability (participation ratio over stem
energy) instead of by genre turns out to admit \~89% of a
genre-stratified 52k corpus. Combined with partial-stem conditioning
that always reconstructs the full mix, this improved plain text-to-music
by ear on the exact prompts that were worst at baseline.

EVALUATION The bleed axis, and how to see it Subject-clone bleed ---
background people turning into the trained subject --- is invisible to
face-recognition scoring, which grades only the largest face. We built
three complementary instruments (a calibrated VLM clone-counting critic,
a DINOv2 structural Subject-Collapse-Rate, and secondary-face detection)
and they reverse the ranking: the ArcFace champion has the second-worst
structural bleed, above the plain baseline. Any identity result reported
on face-similarity alone is reporting half the frontier.

# Identity & digital twins

Face loss, REPA, the decode-free latent probe, and an honest accounting
of what beats a plain LoRA. Face-identity auxiliary losses in video LoRA
training IN PROGRESS The original thread: add explicit identity
supervision to Wan2.1 / 2.2 LoRA video training for digital twins,
because vanilla flow-matching loss correlates with identity preservation
but never directly supervises it. Two terms --- ArcFace embedding
distance as primary, masked face-region pixel MSE as secondary ---
computed on a one-step x̂₀ reconstruction from the noise prediction,
since backpropagating through video sampling is intractable.
Timestep-gated to the lower half of the schedule, with step gating and
warmup as throughput knobs. Built in ai-toolkit, chosen over
diffusion-pipe and musubi-tuner because Wan reuses
SDTrainer.calculate_loss() , so batch tensors and the VAE are already at
the loss site. A related pixel-space result shipped separately: a
reference-conditioned face-crop detailer on HiDream-O1, which corrects
identity drift after a refiner pass. Chosen because a face detailer
needs high-frequency fidelity that a VAE smears, and because O1's
reference pathway is in-context tokens --- so a different-pose reference
works by design. Phase 1a overfit smoke passed cleanly. LatentID:
decode-free identity supervision VALIDATED The flagship mechanism. A
small frozen network reads the VAE latent of a face and outputs a 512-d
identity embedding distilled from a pixel-space recogniser; the loss is
1 − cos(probe(x̂₀), probe(GT)) , sigma-gated, with no decode per step.
Decode-free is a convenience on images and a hard requirement on video.
Findings that made it work: The VAE preserves identity nearly perfectly.
Z-Image's 16-channel f8 VAE has an LFW reconstruction ceiling of 0.867
ArcFace, versus the 0.869 pixel teacher --- so a decode-free probe is
feasible at all. Architecture matters more than capacity. A flatten head
generalises; average pooling destroys the spatial identity code entirely
(the v1 collapse). Data scale is the gate. 6k faces overfit (val-cos
0.10); 80k FFHQ with horizontal flip and noise augmentation generalises
(0.66 on Z-Image, 0.77 on Qwen --- Qwen latents carry identity better).
Loss weight does not transfer between models. Z-Image's 0.1 over-pulls
on Qwen's stronger probe into a step-accumulating degenerate mode; 0.03
is clean. Stronger signal wants proportionally lower weight.

THE HONEST VERDICT On image identity, auxiliary losses do not beat a
well-configured plain LoRA. Clean-inference A/B on an independent engine
with held-out prompts and multiple seeds: all arms land 0.585--0.591
overall, statistically indistinguishable, with the plain baseline
winning two of three prompt families. The earlier "REPA is the identity
champion" headline was n=2--4 training-sample noise; the later "no arm
beats baseline" was a checkpoint-selection artifact at 6k steps. What
survived both corrections: auxiliary losses lock identity 2--3× faster,
and bleed ramps monotonically with steps. So the recipe is aux loss +
early stop --- you reach the identity you want while background bleed is
still flat. Baseline locks so slowly it only reaches comparable identity
deep in the bleed ramp. The portable wins Two config-only fixes turned
out to matter more than any loss: Class-anchored captions. An invented
token alone carries no gender or context prior, so on a strong-prior
scene ("business presentation", "mountain hike") the base scene prior
wins and renders a man. "\[token\] woman" flips both failing prompts to
the correct subject at zero cost on face-centric prompts. Ports to Qwen
and Wan for free. This was isolated by a decisive control: swapping the
invented token for a famous name fixed it, which proved the LoRA is
weight-space and the gap was token-vs-prior, not identity strength.
Checkpoint soup across the identity knee. Uniform-averaging checkpoints
that bracket the knee, no retrain: plain-LoRA bleed 0.355 → 0.240 (−32%)
with identity up. Rule with a mechanism: soup arms whose per-checkpoint
identity is uniform; do not soup an arm whose identity is concentrated
in late high-strength checkpoints, because averaging dilutes it. Rank
discipline. Rank is a real bleed lever --- rank 32 buys no identity and
sharply more bleed (worst structural bleed in the study); rank 8 ≈ rank
16. Exotic parameterisation was not the win. Layered loss.
Decoded-ArcFace positive + InfoNCE over a frozen negative bank, souped
across the knee: identity 0.395 (above plain 0.388) at clone rate 0.267
(below plain 0.290, and 41% below decoded-only 0.455). The positive
holds face similarity; the negatives strip the cloning the positive
would otherwise cause. UNSOLVED Crowd-scene identity bleed is a genuine
weak point. On genuinely crowd-dense prompts the baseline bleed rate is
42--57% regardless of checkpoint, loss, or inference-time masking.
Nothing tried fixes it. It likely needs attention-level per-instance
identity binding, or a product- level guardrail --- not another training
tweak.

# Few-step distillation

rCM ported to two model families that had no public port, plus the
theory of what distillation can and cannot change. rCM × LTX-2.3 video
PARTIAL Porting NVIDIA's rCM to LTX-2.3 for a diversity-preserving
2-step video student. Three consecutive runs collapsed to saturated
green by iteration \~2000 with latent standard deviation climbing
monotonically 0.62 → 2.83. The cause was a double-scaled timestep: rCM's
rectified-flow wrapper already emits c_noise in \[0, 1000\], and the LTX
network then multiplied by its own timestep_scale=1000 , so the
sinusoidal embedding received essentially random values. Wan's network
doesn't multiply --- the bug lived in the seam between rCM's
Wan-conventioned wrapper and LTX's native scaling. Post-fix the
framework produces on-prompt structure with bounded latent statistics.
Pure sCM then plateaus at std ≈ 0.50 against a teacher target of \~0.81
--- compositionally correct but desaturated --- which is the diagnosis
that sCM matches consistency but has no force pushing the student to the
teacher's magnitude. DMD's adversarial critic is the queued next test,
and thanks to the timestep fix it will be the first fair one. A separate
audit later proved the port itself is essentially pixel-perfect
(3--5/255 versus official across the sigma range at the correct scale),
and that the crosshatch "mesh" artifact seen in samples is the signature
of the wrong timestep scale in the sampling recipe, not a model defect.
That distinction saved a model-surgery effort that would have been
wasted. Wan2.2 MoE → few-step image VALIDATED A single 14B consistency
student distilled from the two-expert Wan2.2-T2V-A14B at T=1 . The
design bet: because the combined teacher is "high expert above the
σ-boundary, low expert below", one student trained across the full range
learns step 1 from the high expert and step 2 from the low. Validated:
velocity cosine 0.99984 / 0.99920 against diffusers on the port, JVP
twin tangent cosine 0.999999, 4-step production-grade from \~2k
iterations, 2-step crisp by \~2750. Exported to ComfyUI-native
safetensors with a matching sampler node. A 1024² second run confirmed
the aesthetic conclusion below and produced a genuinely photographic
result at iteration 750 --- plus a hard-won memory lesson: at 1024²/CP=2
the run peaks near 135GB reserved on a 141GB card, and the checkpoint
load is what OOMs on resume (cuBLAS allocates outside torch's caching
allocator, so cached blocks from the load starve the first backward).
Fix is context-parallel 4 plus an explicit cache flush after checkpoint
load.

STRUCTURAL FINDING Pure distillation cannot change aesthetics. The
target is the frozen teacher --- the consistency tangent and the DMD
critic both pull toward it. The only free levers are prompt curation and
teacher CFG. To genuinely restyle, you finetune the teacher's low expert
(where aesthetic and detail live), regenerate teacher data, and
re-distill with the recipe unchanged. Do not swap real-image latents in
as distillation targets (inconsistent with the frozen teacher,
destabilises DMD) and do not diffusion-SFT the student (breaks
consistency). The corollary is that a few-step student is an ideal
target for post-distillation reward methods --- the differentiable chain
is short --- so differentiable reward backprop, Flow-GRPO for non-
differentiable rewards, and Diffusion-DPO all become tractable, provided
they are KL- anchored to the frozen pre-reward student.

# Reflow, base-model SFT, and data mining

Shifting a base model's aesthetic without destroying its competence ---
and finding the data to do it with. Reflow as a de-glamorisation
operator CHARACTERISED Reflow (reference inversion) was expected to fix
"cartooniness". Measured across a checkpoint sweep on neutral prompts,
it does something else and does it monotonically: the base is already
photoreal and cinematic on people, and reflow moves it toward everyday
candid snapshot realism --- brighter, flatter, less staged, with real
skin texture. Sweet spot 250--1000 steps; 2000+ overshoots into flat
amateur-snapshot territory and starts drifting content. This resolved a
real tension. Reflow degrades per-character identity, which is fatal for
a character LoRA --- but it is pure upside at base scale, where there is
no identity to protect. Hence the two-tier strategy: reflow-SFT a base
model for realism and aesthetics, then train character LoRAs on that
improved base. Anchor decay for focused-dataset full finetunes VALIDATED
A full finetune of Z-Image base on a 55k portrait dataset followed the
classic trajectory --- soft through steps 3--7k (the unlearning trough,
where the loss minimum is misleading), sharpening from \~10k --- but
backgrounds kept softening as the model overfit to flat-background
portraits at the expense of base composition competency.
pretrained_anchor_decay: 0.02 drifts each parameter 2% back toward the
original weights every step, against a snapshot taken once at wrap time.
It is now the default for any full finetune of a pretrained image model
on a focused dataset, and must be set from the start --- retrofitting
onto an in-progress run locks the anchor to already- trained weights.
Aesthetic mining without a VLM SHIPPED Finding more images matching a
target aesthetic across S3-scale corpora, without VLM-scoring every
image. Embed once on GPU (CSD style descriptors + LAION aesthetic +
CLIP), then all filtering is instant CPU cosine similarity and
re-runnable for free: multi-prototype style gate, aesthetic floor,
resolution floor, zero-shot subject routing, and k-means diversity
sampling. Delivered 3,386 curated images from three embedded corpora. A
useful diagnostic fell out: the curated reference set was 87% people, so
retrieval skews people by construction --- retrieval quality is
corpus-bound, not metric-bound, which was reconfirmed later when a
fashion corpus turned every interior-moodboard query into sunglasses and
handbags. Packaged as a reusable internal toolkit and a Claude skill so
the team can run it without re-deriving the pipeline.

# Photo → navigable 3D walkthrough

The largest thread: a complete, licence-clean pipeline from one
photograph to a photoreal camera-controlled video walkthrough. This
started from two hard blockers on an existing product path --- the depth
backbone was non-commercial, and the training video was not licensed for
training targets. Both converged on one answer: generate the training
data ourselves, from clean models, with recovered ground truth.
Self-supervised, licence-clean training data VALIDATED The pipeline, per
clip: a clean-provenance start photo → monocular depth cloud → camera-
consistent point tracks projected through a chosen trajectory → LTX-2.3
with the Motion-Track IC- LoRA generates the ground-truth video → a pose
estimator recovers the trajectory the model actually executed → a guide
render is produced at those measured poses → caption → VAE-encode both
sides → train. Two design choices carry it. First, camera control
emerges from object-motion conditioning when the tracks encode real 3D
parallax (see the novelty section). Second --- and this is what makes
the loose control magnitude a non-issue --- we never assume the
commanded trajectory was followed; we measure it and render the guide at
the measured poses, so target and guide stay consistent regardless. A
101° commanded pan that over-rotated to 140° is still a perfectly valid
training pair. Scaled to 2,216 fully-paired clips across 300 real
interior frames × 8 balanced trajectories. The first retrain on it
solved the black-void problem: on the hardest 138° clip the guide is
almost entirely black --- the camera has rotated past the single-image
frustum --- and the output is a fully furnished photoreal interior. The
prior model, trained on a hole-filling depth backbone, could only paint
the void black. Root-causing darkening: a data pathology, not an
inference bug RESOLVED Outputs dimmed progressively across frames. Three
plausible inference explanations were tested and eliminated in order ---
attention trust-masking, VAE tiled decoding, and resolution --- before
scanning all 2,216 training videos revealed the answer: the ground truth
itself darkens, median retain 0.49, with only 11% usable above 0.80. The
generator leans dark because its guide is dots on a dimmed image, so the
student faithfully learned to dim. No inference trick can fix a data
pathology. The fix attacked the cause. Darkening is the model inventing
into uncovered regions; a panorama- anchored cloud has \~full 360°
coverage, so trajectory renders stay covered and there is nothing to
invent. Confirmed: with a pano cloud the output does not darken.

A FINDING THAT GENERALISES The attention trust-mask helps a model that
never learned to fill voids and hurts one that did. Verified by A/B: the
retrained model learned "black void = invent plausible content" from its
training distribution, so handing it a mask that frees the void tokens
is off-distribution and it paints black patches --- the opposite of the
intent. A crutch for one model is a distribution shift for the next. Any
conditioning aid should be re-validated after a retrain, not carried
forward by default. The panorama-anchored geometry stack VALIDATED
Building a metric, seamless point cloud from a single photo required
solving a chain of geometry problems, each of which produced a reusable
result: Seamless depth merge. Per-face monocular depths differ by scale,
shift and shape, so scale- only alignment leaves visible segment
boundaries. The real solve is a gradient-domain Poisson merge in
log-distance, solved sparsely --- one consistent depth field that
reconciles all three. Log gradients are scale-invariant, so absolute
scale must be pinned separately to the metric median. Anchor the gauge,
not a face. A sequential alignment anchored to face 0 collapsed a 3--4.5
m room to \~1 m because face 0 was an outlier. A global least-squares
over all pairwise overlap log-ratios with a median gauge keeps the true
metric scale. Counter-intuitively, more and narrower faces (60° × 12)
give tighter metric consistency than fewer wide ones. Sharpness comes
from pinned real pixels, not splat resolution. Packing the real start
photo into the cloud's observation slots lets the renderer forward-warp
sharp real pixels along the trajectory with depth-based parallax, with
the soft cloud filling only disoccluded edges. Frame 0 becomes the
photograph exactly. Chasing smaller splats buys nothing and costs holes.
Field of view is the recurring trap. Learned focal estimators fail
out-of-distribution on wide architectural renders (all three
underestimated a true \~100° by 30--40°). Two rules came out of it: use
35mm-equivalent focal rather than FOV degrees whenever the render canvas
aspect differs from the photo's, and where two branches disagree (a wide
composite versus the photo's true lens), the visual optimum is their
midpoint --- validated across a 52°--102° spread of scenes. Assert the
determinant. A mirrored room renders completely plausibly and is
near-impossible to catch by eye on soft furnishings. Every coordinate
conversion now asserts a rotation determinant of +1 rather than relying
on visual inspection. The production render SHIPPED The final
configuration composites two renders: a sharp, self-consistent
single-image reconstruction in the covered centre, and the panorama
cloud filling the periphery where the single view has no coverage.
Neither branch has a void, so neither darkens. Roughly 1,000 scenes were
rendered through this at production settings. One bug in that run is
worth recording because it generalises: portrait-aspect scenes rendered
stretched. The cause was feeding the reconstruction model its long edge
at the training resolution, when the model's convention is width-at-518.
For landscape those coincide; for portrait it fed an

off-distribution aspect and the model predicted distorted geometry and
focal. A one-line convention fix, found by tapping each branch's frame 0
separately rather than reasoning about the composite. KEY ARTIFACTS ›

# Editable, persistent worlds

Making a generated world a thing you can change and revisit --- the
write path, the read path, and the decomposition underneath. Thesis: the
world is never frozen, because generation is an operator on the
representation rather than a one-shot render. Edit a room, then fly
through it --- and the edit persists, because it lives in world state.
The read path: point-cloud-conditioned photorealisation VALIDATED
Cosmos 3 finetuned to photorealise a partial point-cloud render along a
camera path. Two findings made it possible. First, the shipped
post-training is vision SFT only with no control-branch recipe --- but
the trainer has a modality-agnostic control path that ingests arbitrary
RGB control and routes it correctly at train time, and the shipped
"video-to-video" mode is only first-frames-of-the-same-clip, so our
cloud→real mapping had to use the control path. Second, an adversarial
audit before scaling found the one real defect: the first frame was
pinned in only 30% of training samples while inference always pins it,
which was the source of weak first-frame adherence. Training data is
fully self-supervised: real clips → recovered poses and depth → fused
cloud → re- render a partial cloud along the recovered path as control,
real clip as target. No manual alignment. Result: wherever the frame-0
cloud has coverage, the model textures faithfully and sharply, near
ground truth. The failure mode is clean and predictable --- large
fully-black reveal regions where the camera exits a doorway render as
iridescent mush, because there is no information there. So it is
excellent for modest walkthrough moves and struggles on big reveals. It
generalised out of domain to high-end architectural renders and
arbitrary trajectories on first test. Action-conditioned object rotation
PARTIAL Two approaches to "rotate this object coherently". The control
route --- mesh-reprojected RGB as control, faithful matte renders as
target, trained on isolated product objects --- is validated and
shipped: it fixed a room-prior shininess artifact, holds coherent
geometry through a full rotation, and converged by iteration 1000. The
numeric action route (start view + per-frame rotation as a registered
action domain) got the model responding strongly to rotation commands
after a data fix, but remains undertrained. That second run also
produced the most instructive debugging sequence of the six months. A
silent wedge was attributed in turn to fork-unsafe video decoding, a
compile recompilation hang, telemetry barriers, and configuration drift
--- each disproven by a controlled test. The actual cause was the first
epoch boundary of a small custom dataset, which explained both observed
wedge points exactly (387 samples ÷ 8 per pack ÷ ranks). The fix is a
cycling dataset. Two general lessons stand: a custom short-clip dataset
in a token-packing trainer must cap samples per batch or the packing
loop runs away, and comparing a failing run against a known-good one on
the same stack is worth more than any number of independent hypotheses.

The write path: an operator table VALIDATED See the novelty section for
the measurements. The ordering results matter as much as the operators:
For appearance, edit the photo first, then lift. The asset inherits the
edit with geometry within 0.7% and no compositing or orientation
problem. Compositing a render back into a photo failed twice --- the
mask is the visible part of a partly-occluded object, and the render
faces a different direction. For geometry, lift first, then edit the
mesh. Enhance and local upscale are a capability gap, not a formulation
problem. A completion LoRA trained on a fixed prompt is a hole-filler
with collapsed text sensitivity: unblanking and lowering denoise removes
the hallucination but makes the operator a near no- op that slightly
smooths, and an explicit weathering instruction changes nothing at any
denoise level. Confirmed by eye, not just by metric. Those operators
need a different model. Object-granular interior inpainting transfers
zero-shot from the object-scale training domain to interior scenes
(masked perceptual distance and structural similarity within noise of
in-domain), so no scene-scale finetune was needed. Flat surface patches
do not --- a model trained to complete objects invents an object in a
bare wall, with predicted texture variance up to 9× ground truth.
EVALUATION CORRECTION Visual review showed object inpainting fills holes
with coherent, seamless, correctly-lit content that is not the
ground-truth content --- it filled a brick wall with white cabinets. For
an authoring tool that is success. So perceptual distance to ground
truth is a fidelity-to- original measure, not an edit-quality measure.
Edit quality needs no-reference or VLM judging plus seam metrics, with
reference metrics reserved for context preservation outside the mask.
Scene decomposition VALIDATED Photo → per-object instances → editable
scene. The pipeline went through three generations, and the arc is the
interesting part: 1. Geometric arbitration. "Depth proposes,
segmentation juries" --- depth-based proposals (protrusion envelopes,
floor-plane height bands) refined by a promptable segmenter, with a
background-plane coplanarity test as the wall filter. A wall is coplanar
by definition; a table protrudes off the floor plane. Clean separation:
walls 0.72--1.00, real objects ≤0.21. 2. Depth-guided segmentation.
Feeding colourised metric depth as a second image alongside RGB to the
segmenter --- no architecture change, just multi-image conditioning ---
natively resolved the three cases the entire geometric scaffolding
existed to patch: a plant became one object instead of a fringe-split,
vases on a table separated, walls fell to background with no coplanarity
gate needed. 3. Semantic arbitration. The persistent failure --- wall
strips, floor slivers, cabinet panels leaking into "foreground" --- is
because "is this liftable?" is a semantic question being answered
geometrically. Reframed: a VLM names freestanding, movable objects; a
concept-promptable

segmenter segments exactly those; everything unnamed is the intact
background shell; depth becomes a tie-breaker rather than the arbiter.
Validated on two scenes --- built-ins correctly left as shell, movable
furniture correctly separated. A separate audit blocked a planned
reinforcement-learning pass with verifiable rewards, for a good reason:
the ground-truth grain is itself fragmented (a kitchen island authored
as nine meshes, a console as top-plus-leg), so the model's
over-splitting is learned and RL would only reinforce it. The durable
fix is a VLM re-labeller producing correct-grain data, then retrain ---
and that re-labeller is the same infrastructure as the reward function.
Texture completion VALIDATED A UV-atlas completion LoRA. The first
formulation was abandoned after being proven broken --- archviz objects
use tiled textures with no single atlas to complete, and the coverage
filter perversely selected tiled junk while rejecting clean unwraps. The
correct formulation uses isolated catalogue objects where the object's
own base-colour texture is the complete-atlas target, with partials
generated by unioning the UV visibility of clustered views. Version 2
plateaued hard at 2000 steps with a specific failure --- flat repeating
textures perfect, structured multi-material objects filled with
plausible generic flat texture instead of reading the references ---
diagnosed as capacity-bound rather than signal-bound. Version 3 used a
staged curriculum by texture edge-energy tercile (mechanics → structure
propagation → reference-driven multi-material fill) at higher rank and
lower learning rate, with each phase gated on evaluation before
launching the next. Each phase did its stated job, and the key
diagnostic case (a brown door frame previously filled beige) came out
correct at phase 2. Two evaluation lessons: judge on the used-region
composite, not the raw sample, since zero- weighted padding correctly
stays flat and makes good samples look bad; and the remaining gap ---
content no source view ever saw --- is fundamental to few-photo
completion and is fixed by inference-time view coverage, not more
training.

I N F E R E N C E R E S E A R C H

# Video-model conditioning mechanics

How these models actually read their conditioning --- attention, RoPE,
masks, and where each one stops working. Multi-keyframe: attention
partitioning, then training VALIDATED The inference-time result is
described in the novelty section. What matters downstream is the
boundary it exposed: inference-time architecture redirects existing
capabilities; it does not add new ones. Multiple keyframes in a tight
window produce wooden, stop-motion output no matter the mask design,
which is why a multi-keyframe trainer exists. That trainer mirrors the
inference contract exactly --- keyframe tokens appended with a RoPE
remap and clean conditioning, loss masked off the keyframe positions ---
so the trained adapter drops into the existing inference path with no
changes. It is validated and the win condition is subtler than expected:
not dramatic content change, but natural in-between motion --- a body
shifting, wind in trees, a character blinking and swallowing before
speaking --- while the anchored composition holds. Data findings that
transfer: corpora win on clip quality, caption consistency and epoch
count rather than on genre; bimodal caption lengths (30 chars from one
source, 1,356 from another) are actively harmful; and a
keyframe-difference gate cheaply filters static talking heads. The
inference recipe accumulated several non-obvious results. An
empty-string negative prompt is required, not a zeroed one --- the
empty-string embedding is the encoder's learned representation of "no
caption", an in-distribution point CFG can pull away from, whereas the
null vector is out-of- distribution and locks multi-keyframe motion.
Ancestral samplers beat deterministic ones because re-injected noise
gives escape hatches out of trajectories that deterministic Euler
reinforces toward the anchors. And a half-resolution first pass
establishes motion that a subsequent upscale-refine fills in without
overriding. MiniMax H3: RoPE-distance conditioning VALIDATED Chunked
video continuation built from scratch, since neither the open weights
nor any template ships one. Beyond the RoPE-distance finding in the
novelty section, two mechanisms proved out. Pinning the handoff frame
matters because context frames only carry whole latent frames (four
pixel frames each), leaving pixel-level ambiguity that shows up as
continuations re-playing an action that already happened; feeding the
prior clip's actual output pixels directly avoids the decode round-trip
and its colour-grade shift. And a reference-influence ramp must be
non-zero --- at zero, any connected reference attends at full strength
across the entire clip with no easing, diluting detail everywhere. Most
"blurry continuation" reports were this setting, not a bug. A
room-grounding adapter was built on top: render a complete panoramic
cloud as a pan-in-place walkthrough, feed it as a context latent, and
the model generates video inside that specific room from text alone. Two
dead ends are worth not repeating --- a learned scene encoder that
matched a latent's shape but was never constrained to match its
distribution (the frozen network only reads content resembling a real
latent, so training found the cheapest nudge: subtle colour shifts), and

discrete cutout still views fed as ordinary reference images, which lack
the shared spatial grid that gives context conditioning its strength.
Conditioning as a continuous signal PARTIAL Two related investigations
into per-pixel control. The upstream union-control adapter's per-pixel
mask behaves as binary in practice --- motion strength 0.01 and 1.0
produce identical motion, and cross-attention spread means any freed
region animates its neighbours regardless of mask tightness. That is a
training-data limitation of the upstream adapter, not something to fix
downstream. The working alternative drops structural conditioning
entirely: encode the rendered frame, inject noise into latent regions
the confidence mask marks uncertain, and denoise with the text prompt as
the only semantic signal in noisy regions. Render supplies structure,
mask supplies where-to- invent, prompt supplies content --- camera
control falls out because the rendered chunks already carry the
trajectory's motion as a temporal prior. The honest caveat: a single
mask cannot serve both "pin the camera" and "fill the void", and
over-tightening the confidence collapses it to near- uniform so flipping
polarity merely toggles all-preserve against all-invent.

A U D I O Masked / separated training for music generation Teaching a
text-to-music trunk how parts relate --- and improving ordinary text-to-
music as a result. The goal was reframed early and correctly: not stem
control, but the quality of ordinary text-to-music generation on exposed
acoustic prompts --- string quartet, piano, folk, flamenco --- which
were muddy and dissonant at baseline. The objective Always reconstruct
the full mix given a random subset of its own separated stems as hints.
Because the target distribution never leaves full-mix, forgetting is
minimal and training stays close to deployment --- and because
evaluation is text-only with no stems fed, any improvement is genuine
trunk transfer rather than conditioning leakage. Two mechanisms were
built on top: a high-noise loss focus (weight the completion loss toward
the timesteps where harmonic commitment actually happens, since harmony
is linearly recoverable at low noise) and a per- frame focus mask on a
rotating withheld line. Results, and how they were read The ablation
ladder was run properly, with a matched control to isolate each factor.
High-noise gating alone is a weak standalone lever --- the clean isolate
is null on metrics. Stem conditioning is the real one. Together they
compound, and the division of labour is legible: MECHANISM IMPROVES
COSTS Partial-stem conditioning De-muddying; richer, more independent
lines Loosens tuning (intonation error +0.74 cents) High-noise loss
focus Tightens commitment and tuning (+0.74 → +0.22) De-muddies less on
its own Both, full stack De-mud, tuning, and texture together --- no
trade-off Non-monotonic; needs early stopping The most valuable result
was root-causing a residual complaint. Wins landed exactly where the
separator works cleanly (funk drums and bass, flamenco plucked guitar);
the residual out-of-tune bowed strings landed exactly where it does not
--- a four-stem separator has no strings class, so string relations were
learned from smeared stems. A separator with real piano and guitar
classes fixed the split (healthy \~55/45 energy rather than the \~1000:1
of every failed attempt). A later

artifact --- "strings follow the piano line too much" --- had the same
root cause and separated two axes that had been conflated: fit (coherent
and in tune) versus independence (distinct voices). THE METHODOLOGICAL
RESULT For this task the aesthetic metric panel is actively misleading.
Raw roughness increased in exactly the runs the ear preferred, because
added detail and richness score as dissonance. Metrics dipped through
what turned out to be an unlearn-first phase, and one checkpoint was
cancelled prematurely on that basis --- the correction is in the record.
Standing rule: the ear arbitrates, mud-index and intonation-error are
the trusted quantitative pair, and roughness and the aesthetic panel are
discounted. Independent verification came from spectrograms of separated
generated audio, which found something no metric saw: on one track a
real, continuous guitar part emerges in the trained model that is
essentially absent in the base, while the horn stem simultaneously
becomes sparser --- a genuinely new independent voice, plus content
reallocation. Also shipped, as the origin of the thread: a company-wide
live radio station --- continuous generation broadcast as one
synchronised stream with a prompt queue and beat-matched DJ transitions,
on a fully permissive stack (a non-commercial beat tracker and three GPL
time- stretchers were ruled out and replaced). The transition pipeline
is tempo-lock with octave folding, phase-lock onto the swap downbeat
rather than the overlap start, a sharp rhythm-section drop so two grids
never play at once, and a low-noise audio-to-audio pass over the seam to
unify timbre.

# Commercial-clean model stack

Licence posture as an engineering constraint, applied consistently
rather than deferred. A recurring, load-bearing constraint: several of
the strongest available components are non- commercial, and the
restriction frequently extends to outputs and to anything distilled from
them. Rather than defer this, each workstream carries a verified verdict
and, where needed, a swap. Depth backbone swapped from a non-commercial
reconstructor to an Apache-licensed one across the walkthrough stack ---
which turned out to be more than a substitution, because the replacement
has no native Gaussian head and only splats visible pixels, producing
hard frustum edges where the original hallucinated soft fill. That
difference is precisely what forced the retrain that later solved the
black-void problem. The successor model was checked and rejected: non-
commercial, with the restriction extending to outputs. Training-data
provenance. The camera trajectories of a large interior dataset are
usably licensed; the video frames are not. That single distinction drove
the entire synthetic-data pipeline --- reuse the trajectories as motion
priors, generate the targets. Background removal, DJ tooling, 3D
generators, world models each carry a verdict; non- commercial and
copyleft components were replaced with permissive equivalents rather
than carved out for internal use. A background-removal model was swapped
to a permissive one; a non-commercial beat tracker and three GPL audio
libraries were replaced with a permissive stack; a jurisdiction-excluded
family of world models was ruled out for one project while remaining
available to another. Distillation inherits licences. The identity
probe's ship blocker is exactly this: a probe distilled from a
non-commercial recogniser inherits its restriction, so clearance
requires training our own recogniser with a permissively-licensed
framework on licensed or synthetic data. The practical consequence is
that the walkthrough deliverable has clean provenance end to end ---
model, backbone, and training data --- which is unusual for work in this
area and is a real differentiator.
